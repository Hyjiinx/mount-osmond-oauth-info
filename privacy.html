Privacy Policy

Mount Osmond Golf Booking is a privately developed application used by its owner to assess potential golf booking opportunities.

Information accessed

With the owner's authorisation, the application accesses Google Calendar event information, including event times, titles and locations, to determine when the owner may be available to play golf. The requested Google Calendar permissions may also permit the application to create or modify calendar events.

Use of information

Calendar information is used to evaluate scheduling availability and support the owner's golf booking workflow. It is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials and application data are intended to be stored on systems controlled by the owner. The application does not operate a public service for other users. Information may be transmitted to Google as needed to use the Google Calendar API. Any additional disclosure is limited to what the owner expressly configures or authorises.

Retention and deletion

The owner can delete locally stored application data and credentials and revoke Google access through Google Account connections.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data handling changes.
